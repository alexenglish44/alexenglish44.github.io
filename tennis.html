Tennis

Thirteen years, one racket.

From local courts in Perth to national titles and NCAA Division I competition.

How it started

I started playing tennis at seven years old. What began as a fun activity quickly became a serious commitment.

Achievements

  • Under-18 Scottish National Champion
  • International junior doubles title winner (ITF Junior Circuit)
  • Represented Scotland multiple times in junior teams across several age groups
  • Now competing for Bellarmine University at NCAA Division I level

What tennis has taught me

Tennis has taught me discipline, resilience, and how to handle pressure. It's an individual sport, but doubles and team events showed me how much trust and communication matter. Balancing training, travel, and a 4.0 GPA has taught me time management that I'll use in any career.
