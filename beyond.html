Beyond the Classroom

What I do when I'm not on court.

Football, live music, and a few favorite stories.

Football

I'm a football (soccer) fan through and through, and I support two clubs: Arsenal and Celtic. Whether it's a Premier League match or a Scottish derby, I love the atmosphere and the passion of match day. Supporting Celtic is also a nice link back to home.

Music festivals

Live music is one of my favorite ways to unwind, and festivals are the best way to enjoy it, with great atmosphere and great people.

TV shows and movies

I'm a big fan of The Lord of the Rings, and I love the world-building, the storytelling, and the idea of a small group taking on something enormous. On the lighter side, Suits is a favorite. The sharp dialogue and business strategy fit nicely with my interest in entrepreneurship.
